## 1. Scope and provisional definitions

This draft proposes Enterprise-only eligibility for SLA service credits across
the six-tier rate card. It addresses only uptime, p99 GET latency, DSR erasure,
and billing reconciliation, which are the measures with an existing credit
schedule in the historical SLA. It adds no credits for synthetic-page or BYOK
targets. Those targets are not treated here as verified capabilities or
credit-eligible measures.

The historical v1.0.0 names “HuGR Labs” as Provider. That name is carried here
only as an inherited draft label; Counsel must verify the contracting legal
entity before publication. This draft makes no representation that a GitHub
organization is the Provider.

The Enterprise reference targets carried from v1.0.0 §2 are uptime ≥ 99.95%,
p99 GET latency < 300 ms, DSR erasure ≤ 30 days, and billing reconciliation
drift < 0.1% at 24 hours. These are draft targets, not evidence of achieved
performance or provider readiness. Their publication remains subject to Counsel

this draft for the synthetic-page or BYOK kill-switch targets in v1.0.0.

## 2. Proposed Enterprise credit schedule

The following preserves the historical percentages while making adjacent bands
non-overlapping. It is not effective until the gates in §5 pass.

### 2.1 Uptime / availability

Let `shortfall` be the difference in percentage points between the Enterprise
target and measured monthly uptime. Apply the catastrophic rule first. Otherwise:

| Measured result | Credit as % of Monthly Service Fee |
|---|---:|
| Target met or exceeded | 0% |
| `0 < shortfall < 0.5 pp` | 5% |
| `0.5 pp ≤ shortfall < 1.0 pp` | 10% |
| `1.0 pp ≤ shortfall < 2.5 pp` | 25% |
| `2.5 pp ≤ shortfall ≤ 5.0 pp` | 50% |
| `shortfall > 5.0 pp` or absolute uptime `< 95%` | 100% plus the §4 termination right |

Catastrophic uptime takes priority over every lower band. The 100% credit remains
subject to the aggregate cap in §3; the termination right is additional.

### 2.2 p99 GET latency

An excess must be sustained for at least one hour. For positive excess over the
approved Enterprise target:

| Excess over target | Credit as % of Monthly Service Fee |
|---|---:|
| `0 < excess ≤ 25%` | 5% |
| `25% < excess ≤ 50%` | 10% |
| `excess > 50%` | 25% |

### 2.3 Freshness

| Breach | Credit |
|---|---|
| DSR erasure `30 days ≤ duration < 45 days` | 5% |
| DSR erasure `duration ≥ 45 days` | 25% plus DPO incident review |
| Billing reconciliation drift `≥ 0.1%` sustained for `> 24 hours` | 10% |

No additional freshness target or credit is created by this draft.

## 3. Stacking, cap, and remedy

Credits stack only across distinct SLOs in the same Service Period. Their
aggregate is capped at 100% of the Monthly Service Fee. A catastrophic uptime
breach may produce a 100% credit and the termination right described in §4.

When this draft later becomes effective, the credits specified for covered
Enterprise SLO breaches are the sole and exclusive remedy for those breaches,
except for the §4 termination right and any Provider indemnification obligations
under the applicable master agreement. No separate refund is added for the same
ordinary SLA breach.

A signed Enterprise Order Form may change only the metrics, rates, or remedies
that it expressly identifies. No Order Form is presumed to exist, and Counsel
must approve the precedence wording before publication.

## 4. Termination and prepaid-fee refund

The historical v1.0.0 §8 termination criteria are carried forward for review:
three consecutive months of uptime breach against an applicable §2 SLO, one
catastrophic uptime breach, or repeated DSR erasure breach in two consecutive
months. A pro-rata refund of prepaid fees applies only after a valid termination
under this section and is not a second recovery for the same SLA breach. Counsel
must approve the final cross-reference and wording before publication.
