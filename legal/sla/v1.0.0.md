## 2. Uptime SLO per Tier

| Tier | Uptime target (monthly) | Latency target | Freshness target | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | ≥ 99.0% | p99 GET < 800 ms | DSR ≤ 60 d | Best-effort; no service credits below. |
| **Starter** | ≥ 99.5% | p99 GET < 500 ms | DSR ≤ 30 d; Billing reconciliation < 0.1% drift @ 24 h | Service credits per §4. |
| **Pro** | ≥ 99.9% | p99 GET < 300 ms | DSR ≤ 30 d; Billing reconciliation < 0.1% drift @ 24 h | Service credits per §4. |
| **Enterprise** | ≥ 99.95% | p99 GET < 300 ms | DSR ≤ 30 d; Billing reconciliation < 0.1% drift @ 24 h; Synthetic page p99 < 5 min; BYOK unavailable; no kill-switch SLO | Service credits per §4 + enhanced remedies. |

All SLOs trace to canonical IDs in SLO-CATALOG:
- `SLO-AVAIL-CAS-PUT` (availability — PUT path).
- `SLO-AVAIL-CAS-GET` (availability — GET path).
- `SLO-LAT-CAS-GET` (latency — GET path, p99).
- `SLO-FRESH-DSR-ERASURE` (erasure freshness).
- `SLO-FRESH-BILLING` (billing reconciliation drift).
- `SLO-INCIDENT-RESPONSE-SYNTHETIC-PAGE` (Enterprise only).

## 3. Measurement methodology

1. **Source of truth.** Uptime, latency, and freshness are measured by the Provider-operated
   observability stack (Prometheus + Grafana per region) and corroborated by Customer-facing
   synthetic probes documented in `docs/site/observability/synthetic-probes.md`.
2. **Aggregation.** Per-region values are aggregated to a global monthly value using the
   weighted-by-request-count method. Customer is entitled to per-region breakdown on request.
3. **Customer access.** Provider publishes a per-tenant monthly SLA report on the customer
   dashboard within 10 business days of the close of each Service Period.

---

## 4. Service-credit policy

Service credits are calculated as a percentage of the **Monthly Service Fee** for the affected
Service Period and issued automatically against the next invoice. Credit tiers stack only across
distinct SLOs (i.e., breaches across availability and latency in the same month are summed,
capped per §4.4).

### 4.1 Uptime / availability credits

| Tier-target uptime achieved | % of Monthly Service Fee credited |
|---|---|
| Target met or exceeded | 0% |
| Target − 0.5 percentage points (e.g., Pro 99.4–99.89%) | **5%** |
| Target − 0.5 to 1.0 pp (e.g., Pro 98.9–99.39%) | **10%** |
| Target − 1.0 to 2.5 pp | **25%** |
| Target − 2.5 to 5.0 pp | **50%** |
| Catastrophic (> 5.0 pp below target, or `< 95%` absolute) | **100% + termination right** |

### 4.2 Latency credits (p99 GET > target)

| Excess over target | % of Monthly Service Fee credited |
|---|---|
| ≤ 25% over target sustained ≥ 1 hour | **5%** |
| 25–50% over target sustained ≥ 1 hour | **10%** |
| > 50% over target sustained ≥ 1 hour | **25%** |

### 4.3 Freshness credits (DSR / billing)

| Breach | Credit |
|---|---|
| DSR erasure ≥ 30 d and ≤ 45 d | **5%** |
| DSR erasure ≥ 45 d | **25%** + DPO incident review |
| Billing reconciliation drift ≥ 0.1% sustained > 24 h | **10%** |

### 4.4 Cap

The aggregate of all service credits in any single Service Period is capped at **100%** of the
Monthly Service Fee, except where catastrophic breach occurs (§4.1 last row) in which case the
termination right under §8 applies in addition to the 100% credit.

### 4.5 Exclusive remedy

Service credits issued under this §4 are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any breach of
the uptime, latency, or freshness commitments in §2, except as expressly stated in §8
(termination) and except for Provider's indemnification obligations (if any) under the master
agreement.

## 5. Force majeure carve-outs

Provider is not in breach of this SLA when unavailability is directly caused by any of:

1. **Force majeure** — acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, government action, regional
   internet backbone outages affecting > 25% of public traffic to the relevant Region.
2. **Customer-controlled events** — Customer misuse, Customer's own infrastructure failures,
   integrations the Customer chose to enable outside CoreLink boundaries.
3. **Upstream provider outages** — provided Provider followed `PAT-REGION-FAILOVER-001`
   reasonable failover steps and the upstream outage affects > 50% of comparable cloud-region
   capacity.
4. **Scheduled maintenance** — per §6.
5. **Beta or preview features** — explicitly labeled as such in the product UI or docs.

Provider must document any force-majeure carve-out applied to a Service Period in the monthly
SLA report.

---

## 6. Scheduled maintenance window

1. **Standard window.** Sundays 06:00–08:00 UTC, capped at **4 hours per calendar quarter**.
2. **Notice.** Minimum **7 days** advance notice via the customer dashboard and email.
3. **Emergency maintenance.** Provider may perform emergency security maintenance with as much
   notice as practicable; emergency maintenance is counted toward downtime if it exceeds 30 min
   in any single Service Period, unless §5 applies.
4. **Enterprise tier.** Provider will coordinate maintenance windows with Enterprise customers
   on request to align with customer change-freeze calendars.

---

## 7. Claim procedure

To claim a service credit, the Customer must submit a written claim:

1. **Channel.** `support@humangr.com` (Free/Starter/Pro) or the dedicated Enterprise success
   channel. Subject line: `SLA claim — <tenant_id> — <YYYY-MM>`.
2. **Window.** Within **30 calendar days** of the end of the Service Period in which the
   breach occurred. Claims submitted later are time-barred.
3. **Content.** Tenant ID, affected SLO ID(s), incident window(s), and any corroborating evidence
   (Customer-side observability, ticket IDs, screenshots).
4. **Acknowledgement.** Provider acknowledges within **5 business days** and posts a
   determination within **15 business days**.
5. **Disputes.** Customer may escalate disputes through the SRE Lead and, if unresolved, the
   Customer Success VP. Unresolved disputes proceed to mediation under §9.
6. **Automatic credits.** Service credits identified by Provider's own monthly SLA report do not
   require a claim and are applied automatically against the next invoice.

Internal procedural reference: `specs/_runbooks/RB-SLA-CREDIT.md` (linked from §13).

## 8. Termination right

Customer may terminate the underlying agreement for material breach of this SLA, without
penalty and with pro-rata refund of any prepaid fees, if any of the following occurs:

1. **Three consecutive months** of uptime breach against §2 in the same SLO.
2. **Catastrophic breach** as defined in §4.1 last row in any single Service Period.
3. **Repeated DSR erasure breach** (§4.3 second row) in **two consecutive months**.

Customer must give 30 days written notice and Provider has 30 days to cure (where curable).

## 9. Governing law and jurisdiction

1. **Default governing law.** Laws of the State of Delaware, USA, excluding conflict-of-laws
   rules, except where local mandatory law (LGPD in Brazil, GDPR in the EEA/UK) applies.
2. **Venue.** State and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, USA, except that
   Customer may bring claims relating exclusively to LGPD or GDPR compliance in the customer's
   home jurisdiction.
3. **Dispute resolution.** Parties will first attempt good-faith negotiation for 30 days,
   then non-binding mediation (one mediator agreed in writing or appointed by JAMS), before
   any litigation.
4. **EU / Brazil customers.** Provider acknowledges Schrems II considerations and the LGPD
   `Art. 33` international-transfer regime; see DPA §11 for SCC reference.
5. **Public sector / regulated industries.** Negotiable per pricing addendum.

## 10. Reporting

1. **Monthly SLA report.** Per-tenant, available via customer dashboard within 10 business
   days of Service Period close. Includes per-SLO achievement, applicable credits, and force-
   majeure carve-outs.
2. **Quarterly review.** Provider's Customer Success conducts a quarterly review with
   Enterprise customers, including trend analysis and improvement commitments.
3. **Public status page.** `https://hugrl.betteruptime.com` provides real-time incident and degradation
   notifications.
