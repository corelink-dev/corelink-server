Any other account, Worker, path or method is refused, and so are the production and staging D1 UUIDs. The protected job binds `CF_I2568_API_TOKEN` to the repository deploy token `CF_API_TOKEN`, as #2880, #2884 and #2885 did, and `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` to the pinned account. The pre-merge candidate gate still pins the exact bytes.
