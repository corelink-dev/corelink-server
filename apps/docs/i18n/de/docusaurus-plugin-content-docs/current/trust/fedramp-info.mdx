# FedRAMP posture

> **TL;DR — CoreLink does NOT pursue FedRAMP authorization today.** Our
> SOC 2 Type I (target Q1-2027) + ISO 27001:2022 (target Q1-2027) program
> crosswalks to **17 of the 18 FedRAMP Rev 5 Moderate control families
> substance** across 17 of 18 NIST 800-53 control families. **If your
> procurement requires a FedRAMP-authorized vendor, please contact
> `enterprise@humangr.com` for a sponsorship discussion** — we will
> re-open the FedRAMP decision under named-customer sponsorship terms.

## What this page tells you

- We are **not on the FedRAMP Marketplace** and do not appear in
  `marketplace.fedramp.gov`.
- We do **not** hold a Joint Authorization Board P-ATO or an Agency ATO.
- We are not pursuing one in the 2026-2027 GA window.
- We **do** maintain an informational crosswalk so that you can ground-truth
  our SOC 2 + ISO 27001 posture against the NIST 800-53 Rev 5 Moderate
  baseline you may already know.

## Why we are not pursuing FedRAMP today

The full rationale (with cost / timeline numbers and trigger conditions) is
in the internal companion document
`FEDRAMP-NOT-IN-SCOPE-RATIONALE.md`, available on request from `trust@humangr.com`.
Summary:

| Reason | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Market** | Our GTM target is commercial SaaS engineering — not US federal. No federal pipeline as of 2026-05-15. |
| **Cost** | $500-900k all-in first-year (3PAO + SSP authoring + FIPS module uplift + personnel screening + ConMon). |
| **Timeline** | 12-18 months wall-clock to ATO (Agency path) or 24-36 months (JAB path). Incompatible with our Q4-2026 GA window. |
| **Substance already covered** | 17 of the 18 Moderate control families have their substance in place today via SOC 2 + ISO 27001 — the cert itself is the only delta, and it's a procurement gate, not a controls gate. |

### What's not covered

- **PS — Personnel Security:** FedRAMP Moderate requires US-citizen background-investigated personnel for privileged access. We do not implement OPM-style screening; we implement SOC 2 / ISO 27001 personnel-security controls (Owner attestation + advisor pool CV review + NDAs).
- **IA-7 + SC-13 cryptographic-module FIPS 140-3 validation:** substance is in place across our BYOK matrix; AWS KMS is FIPS 140-3 Level 1 attested today (per
  BYOK FIPS attestation matrix, available on request from `trust@humangr.com`);
  GCP CloudHSM, Azure Key Vault HSM Premium, and HashiCorp Vault Enterprise FIPS paths are mapped with RFIs in flight.
- **IR-6 US-CERT reporting clause:** the FedRAMP-specific 1-hour reporting timeline to US-CERT is not part of our IR plan. We do commit to 72-hour breach notification under LGPD Art. 33 / GDPR Art. 33 to customers and supervisory authorities.
- **PL-2 FedRAMP SSP template:** our security/privacy plan is documented exhaustively in
  `security_model.md`
  and
  `privacy_model.md`
  — not in the GSA SSP Word template.
- **CA-6 ATO process:** by definition not in scope when we do not pursue authorization.

## When the FedRAMP decision would re-open

We re-evaluate the not-in-scope decision under any of the following **hard trigger** conditions (full list in
`FEDRAMP-NOT-IN-SCOPE-RATIONALE.md §4`, available on request from `trust@humangr.com`):

1. **Named US federal customer with sponsorship.** A US federal agency, DoD component, or federal system integrator commits in writing to sponsor a FedRAMP authorization with one or more of: Agency ATO sponsorship, $1.5M+ ARR or $5M+ TCV contract, $400k+ co-funding of 3PAO + SSP costs, 12-18-month timeline acceptance.
2. **Aggregate federal pipeline ≥ $5M ARR** across three or more opportunities each conditioned on FedRAMP authorization.
3. **Industry partner co-investment** of ≥ $500k toward FedRAMP authorization as part of a partnership / OEM agreement.

Soft triggers (revisited at quarterly compliance review):

- $500k+ ACV commercial enterprise prospect explicitly making FedRAMP a procurement-blocking requirement.
- Cloudflare Workers / R2 / D1 / DO obtains FedRAMP Moderate authorization on the Marketplace.
- FedRAMP Rev 6 baseline release materially changes substance.
- Adjacent certification needed (DoD CMMC, IRS Pub 1075, CJIS).
