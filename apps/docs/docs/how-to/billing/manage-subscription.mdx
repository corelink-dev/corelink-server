# Manage your subscription

CoreLink uses **Stripe** to process payments. To update your payment
method, download past invoices, change your plan, or cancel, you'll
work inside the secure **Stripe Customer Portal** — a hosted UI we
launch from inside the CoreLink admin UI.

## When to use this guide

You're an `Owner` on a CoreLink tenant with an active paid
subscription, and you need to:

- Update or replace the credit card on file (card expired, fraud
  alert, switching cards).
- Download a PDF copy of an invoice for finance / accounts payable.
- Move between Free, Starter, Team, and Enterprise tiers.
- Switch between monthly and annual billing.
- Update your billing address or tax ID for invoice compliance.
- Cancel your subscription.

If you only need to **view** your current plan or read-only invoice
metadata, the customer dashboard (`/en/customer/dashboard`) shows it
without opening the portal.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as `Owner` on the tenant. (Admins can see the
  billing page but cannot open the portal — Stripe's portal grants
  destructive actions like cancellation, which only Owner can
  approve.)
- Your tenant has completed Checkout at least once. New tenants on
  the free tier won't see the portal button until they upgrade.

## Open the portal

1. Sign in to CoreLink admin UI at
   `https://humangr.com/corelink`.
2. Navigate to **Customer → Billing**
   (`/en/customer/billing`).
3. Click **Open Stripe Portal**.

You'll be redirected to `billing.stripe.com` in the same browser
window. The redirect is one-shot and signed — the URL is valid for
about five minutes after you click. If you wait too long and the
URL expires, just come back to the billing page and click the
button again.

## What you can do in the portal

Inside `billing.stripe.com` you'll see four panels:

### Invoices

Past 24 months of invoices, sorted newest-first. Click any row to
download the PDF. The PDF carries your billing address, our tax
information, and a unique invoice number suitable for accounts
payable.

### Payment methods

Add, remove, or update credit cards. Setting a new default takes
effect on your next renewal automatically — there's no separate
"save" step you need to make in CoreLink.

> **Card data note.** Card numbers, CVCs, and expiry dates are
> entered directly into Stripe's hosted form; CoreLink never sees
> them. This keeps us inside **PCI-DSS SAQ-A** scope, which is one of
> the reasons we use Stripe instead of building our own card form.

### Subscription

Switch tiers (Free ↔ Starter ↔ Team ↔ Enterprise) and switch
between monthly and annual billing.

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately and prorate the current
  period.
- **Downgrades** schedule at the end of the current billing period
  (so you don't lose paid-for capacity early).
- **Annual ↔ monthly switches** trigger a proration that Stripe
  computes and shows you before you confirm.

**Enterprise customers:** self-service downgrade out of Enterprise
is disabled in the portal because Enterprise plans carry a paper
contract + DPA. Click the **Contact sales** link in the portal
instead — that opens an email to `sales@humangr.com` so we can
handle the contract amendment together.

### Cancellation

Click **Cancel subscription** to schedule a cancellation. Two
options:

- **Cancel at period end** (default). Your subscription stays
  active through the end of the current billing period; no refund
  for the unused portion of that period; auto-renew is OFF.
- **Cancel immediately.** Subscription ends today. Stripe will
  prorate the unused portion and refund automatically.

Cancellation does NOT delete your tenant or your cached data.
Your tenant drops to the **Free tier** at the cancellation effective
date. To fully delete your tenant + all customer data, see
[Submit a deletion request](/how-to/leave-corelink)
(GDPR / LGPD compliant).

## When you return to CoreLink

When you click **Return to CoreLink** in the portal (or close the
portal tab and reopen `https://humangr.com/corelink`), you land back on
`/en/customer/billing`. Any changes you made in the portal —
plan changes, card updates, cancellations — are already reflected
because Stripe pushes them to us as webhook events the moment they
happen.

If you see stale data on the CoreLink side (for example, you upgraded but
the dashboard still shows the old tier), wait 30 seconds and
refresh. If the discrepancy persists past 5 minutes, file a ticket
— it likely means a webhook delivery failed and our DLQ replay
runbook needs to run (we'll handle it on our side).

## What's separate from the CoreLink admin UI

The Stripe Customer Portal is **only** for the billing surface.
Everything below stays inside CoreLink admin UI and is NOT manageable
in the Stripe Portal:

| Topic                                | Where it lives                                    |
|--------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Usage metering (bytes stored, hits)  | `/en/customer/dashboard`                          |
| BYOK encryption key management       | `/en/customer/byok`                               |
| Team / role / PAT management         | `/en/customer/team`                               |
| API tokens (PAT issuance + revoke)   | `/en/customer/tokens`                             |
| Region selection + DPA addendum      | `/en/customer/region` and `/en/legal/dpa`         |
| Audit log of admin actions           | `/en/admin/audit` (Admins / Owners only)          |

## Troubleshooting

### "Open Stripe Portal" button does nothing

Open your browser's developer console. If you see a 5xx response on
`/api/v1/customer/billing/portal-session`, our portal endpoint is
having trouble. Try again in a minute. If the failure persists,
check our [status page](https://hugrl.betteruptime.com) and file a
ticket.

### Portal opens but shows "You haven't configured a customer portal yet"

This is a CoreLink-side misconfiguration. File a ticket immediately
with a screenshot — our on-call follows
`RB-STRIPE-PORTAL-INCIDENT` to fix.

### I'm asked to sign in to Stripe inside the portal

That's expected for **high-risk actions** (like changing the
default card or cancelling). Stripe sends a one-time code to your
billing email — enter it to continue. The CoreLink admin UI session
and the Stripe-side reauth are independent, by design.

### Cancellation showed but my tenant is still on the paid tier

Cancellations default to "at period end." You'll stay on your paid
tier until the period ends, then drop to Free automatically. If you
need to leave the paid tier immediately, choose **Cancel
immediately** in the portal — Stripe will refund the prorated unused
portion.
